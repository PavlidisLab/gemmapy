## Why

swagger-codegen v3's Python generator cannot express a response-level
`oneOf`. It emits an empty `InlineResponseNNN` model, which makes the raw
SDK return a plain dict and breaks every `ps.process_*(response.data)` call
in the wrapper.

hundreds of request-body models the wrapper never calls, and yields a
client able to mutate curation state. gemma.R filters the same way in
`inst/script/registry.R`.
